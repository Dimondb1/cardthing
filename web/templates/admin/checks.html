Claude reads the rows the free checks leave and says whether two things are the same product. In trial it only suggests: its answer shows under each row. Once you agree with its answers, tap Let Claude act. When it is sure a cheapest price is the right product, it ticks the row off as checked: the price stays on the site with no saving claimed, and the row comes back if the price or the shop's title changes. When it is sure a price or a shop's page is another product, it hides it or says no. It never counts a price as right and never merges by itself, and each answer it leaves for you says why. Each act is listed under Sorted for you with Undo, and if you undo three in a week it goes back to suggesting.
